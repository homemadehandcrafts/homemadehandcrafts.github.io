Our story

A Little Beauty in Every Detail

At Zeyna, we believe that the smallest details can make everyday moments feel special.

Our handmade jewelry celebrates individuality, creativity, and the beauty of thoughtful design. Each piece is created with diligence and care, combining carefully selected pearls, beads, and other materials into distinctive accessories that complement your personal style.

From understated elegance to playful pops of color, our designs are made for women who appreciate jewelry with character.

Every piece begins with an idea, takes shape through careful craftsmanship, and is created to bring a little more beauty to your day.

Made for everyday wear

No special occasion needed. A few easy ways to wear Zeyna.

Stack your bangles

Wear two or three together and mix wood, pearl and color. They look best in a little crowd.

Layer your necklaces

Pair a twisted strand with a pendant on cord for an easy weekend look.

Wear pearls with denim

Pearls don't need a dress code. Try them with a white tee, jeans and your favorite sneakers.

Made with Diligence. Created with Care.

At Zeyna, handmade means more than simply putting materials together. It means giving attention to the details that make every design special.

Each jewelry piece is individually designed and handcrafted with patience, care, and a thoughtful eye for detail. Our use of pearls, beads, colors, and textures brings a distinctive character to every creation.

Because our jewelry is handmade, individual pieces may have subtle variations that make each creation unique.

Thoughtfully designed. Carefully handcrafted. Made to be cherished.
